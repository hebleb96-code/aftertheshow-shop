Store policy

Privacy

After the Show is a Los Angeles men’s streetwear shop. This is a short, plain-language note about what we collect — not a law-firm document.

What we collect

  • Emails for drop alerts, through a Google Form that lands in a Google Sheet.
  • Order details when you check out. Payments are processed by Stripe.
  • Site analytics through Google Analytics (measurement ID G-EFZYL7ZCVF).

What we don’t do

We do not sell your personal data. We don’t have a Meta pixel installed. If you’re in California: we don’t sell or share personal information for ads — not yet, and not now.

Who else sees it

Stripe handles card and checkout data. Google sees drop-alert emails (Form/Sheet) and anonymous-ish analytics traffic. That’s the list.
